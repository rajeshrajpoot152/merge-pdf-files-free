Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last updated: September 2026

Core Privacy Principle: MergePDFFilesFree executes all PDF merging and splitting operations entirely in your local browser memory. Your files are never uploaded to our servers, never stored, and never accessible by us or any third party.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

When you use our tools to merge or split PDF files:

  • We do not transmit your documents to external web servers.
  • We do not store, copy, inspect, or retain your PDF documents.
  • We do not extract metadata, text, or images from your documents.
  • We do not require user account registration, passwords, email addresses, or payment details.

2. In-Browser Client-Side Processing

Our web application leverages modern client-side JavaScript APIs and WebAssembly (via PDF-Lib and JSZip). All processing takes place within your browser's sandboxed environment on your computer or mobile device. Once you close your browser tab or click "Clear All", all temporary in-memory buffers are automatically discarded.

3. Log Data and Analytics

Like standard websites, standard non-identifiable web server logs (such as request timestamps, browser user-agent string, and HTTP status codes) may be automatically logged for server health and operational stability. No document contents are ever included in these technical logs.

4. Cookies and Advertising

We do not use tracking cookies to profile user activity. Third-party advertising vendors (such as Google AdSense) may serve ads on our site and may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions or feedback regarding our privacy practices, please contact us at support@mergepdffilesfree.com.
