Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 12, 2026 • Effective Immediately

Fundamental Privacy Guarantee: MergePDFFilesFree operates on a 100% client-side architecture. All document merging, splitting, converting, and compressing happens directly in your browser's local sandbox using WebAssembly and HTML5 APIs. Your documents are never uploaded to any remote web servers, never stored, and never inspected.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Because our utilities execute locally on your hardware, we have no mechanism or desire to access your confidential materials:

  • We do not transmit your PDF documents, images, or metadata to external servers.
  • We do not record, save, archive, or cache your file names, text content, or image data.
  • We do not require user account registration, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • We do not charge subscription fees, eliminating the need to collect credit card or billing information.

2. In-Browser Memory Management

When you select or drag files into our interface, the browser allocates a temporary chunk of random-access memory (RAM). When you close the tab, refresh the page, or click "Clear All", this memory buffer is automatically destroyed by your operating system's garbage collector.

3. Web Analytics and Server Logs

Our web hosting servers may record anonymous, aggregated technical metrics including user-agent strings, HTTP request timestamps, and referrer headers solely to diagnose network errors, defend against DDoS attacks, and verify uptime. None of these server logs contain document data.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Cookies

We may partner with third-party advertising vendors such as Google AdSense to display non-intrusive advertisements that keep our service completely free. Third-party vendors may use cookies or web beacons to serve advertisements based on prior visits to this or other websites. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

5. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you retain strict rights over your personal data. Because we do not store, process, or sell any personal data or document content, your privacy rights are structurally preserved by design.

6. Contact Our Data Protection Officer

If you have questions regarding our privacy protocol, email our compliance team at support@mergepdffilesfree.com.
