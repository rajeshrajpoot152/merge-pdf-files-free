How to Convert Photos & Document Scans to PDF on Mobile (iOS & Android)

When applying for visas, job postings, university admissions, or rental leases, applicants are commonly required to submit identity documents, transcripts, and proof of address in PDF format. Snapping photos on your phone is simple, but sending separate JPG or HEIC image files looks unprofessional and often fails portal uploads.

Why Native Phone Conversion is Often Frustrating

While smartphones offer rudimentary PDF printing features, they suffer from major limitations:

  • No Multi-Page Ordering: iPhones and Android devices often create individual 1-page PDFs for every photo instead of grouping them into one unified file.
  • Clunky Margins & Cropping: Default "Print to PDF" menus frequently introduce awkward white borders or cut off document margins.
  • App Store Paywalls: Dedicated mobile scanner apps bombard users with subscription popups (.99/month) or watermark outputs.

The Free In-Browser Solution: 3 Quick Steps

You don’t need to download any third-party apps from the App Store or Google Play. Simply open Safari, Chrome, or Firefox on your phone and use our client-side utility:

  1. Open the Tool: Navigate to MergePDFFilesFree JPG to PDF on your mobile browser.
  2. Select or Take Photos: Tap the blue Select JPG / PNG Images button. You can choose photos directly from your Photo Library or tap "Take Photo" to scan live paper documents.
  3. Arrange & Convert: Tap the arrow buttons to reorder pages sequentially. Tap Convert Images to PDF. Your combined PDF will download automatically to your phone’s Files or Downloads folder.

Pro Photography Tip for Clean PDF Scans:

Place the document on a flat surface with high contrast (e.g., white paper on a dark desk). Ensure daylight or direct overhead light to eliminate hand shadows before capturing.
